Four picks in place, and five verbs for the empty pane.

Picked. One Half Light for the light terminal. The cluster and the legend both carried forward, the legend now keeping its colour at rest. The prompt line as the empty pane's base, with the OS marks from Lobe Icons. New. The legend settled as the gauge edge, its bottom line the context meter. The stopwatch and the corner for stop, resume and fullscreen, outlined and finished, with a glow on the active frame. And the empty pane kept to two jobs, resuming a session and launching an agent, in the picked frame on the picked theme; the verbs that act on other running sessions wait for the composer bar.
What is settled, and what the verbs need
  1. One Half Light.Near-white, cool, the light sibling of Ghostty's ground. Five of its twelve chromatic colours sit under 3:1, all of them brights; a tightened copy below brings those five to 3:1 without touching the rest, so the choice is shipped or tightened.theme
  2. Two frames, one fix.The cluster and the legend both stay, and the legend is now the gauge edge, its bottom line filling with the context used. At rest it lost its pane colour, which the cluster's hollow pill never did; three ways to give it back, one recommended.framesat rest
  3. Marks from Lobe.Apple and Microsoft come from the same Lobe Icons set the agent marks come from. Lobe has no Linux, so Tux comes from Simple Icons, which is the set Lobe's own marks are drawn to match.marks
  4. Resume, or launch.The empty pane's field searches past sessions to pick one up; the chips beneath it launch a new agent. Review, fork and lead act on other running sessions, and the composer bar is the likelier home for them, so they are deferred.quick launchbehaviour

The legend at rest

Four inactive panes, one per way of keeping the colour. First, as it was: the name in ink at 55%, no colour anywhere. Then the name keeps its pane colour at 700 weight and 80%, the frame stays a hairline. Then a 6px dot in the pane colour before an ink name. Then the frame itself takes the pane colour at 45% under an ink name.

Recommended: the name keeps its colour. It matches the cluster's hollow pill, it costs no new element, and across a deck of four the coloured names read as a legend of the swarm. The dot is the fallback if the name's colour ever needs to stay reserved for the active pane.

Part III

Stop, resume, fullscreen

Decided: the stopwatch and the corner, outlined. This part is the finish. The chip and the tab are hairline objects sitting on the frame line, lifted by a one-pixel inner highlight and a whisper of shadow, with colour kept to the one thing that means something: the square is the pane colour, orange under the pointer, a green triangle when there is a session to resume. The active frame gains a soft glow in the pane colour, at three levels so the amount can be chosen. Every legend on the page now wears the settled controls, including the empty panes in Part IV, which show only the corner.

Refined

What changed from the first outlined drawing. The rule drops from 28% to 20% and gains the highlight and shadow, so the chip reads as a made thing rather than a drawn one. Type goes from 700 to 600, tracked a hair, tabular. The square shrinks to 6px with a half-pixel halo in its own colour. Hover no longer colours the whole chip; it deepens the rule, fills the ground by 5%, and turns only the square orange with a small glow, which is what a press will do. The tooltip is sentence case. The corner tab matches the chip exactly: 16px, the same rule, the same lift, the arrows at 70% until hovered, then the pane colour.

08

The glow

The active frame's glow at three levels, in both themes. It is two shadows: an outer one in the pane colour that softens the hairline into the ground, and an inner one that tints the terminal's edge toward it, so the card reads as lit from within rather than outlined. None is the frame as it was. Subtle is the level used everywhere else on this page. Soft is the same recipe at almost twice the strength.

Subtle is my pick: on the near-white ground it is a suggestion, on Ghostty's dark it is the difference between a rule and a light. Soft starts to read as a selection state, which the swarm already owns.

Part IV

The empty pane

Two jobs: pick up a session, or start an agent. The prompt line as picked. The field searches past sessions to resume, this folder first; the agents sit beneath it as chips with their keys for a new launch. Review, fork and lead wait for the composer bar. The overlay appears on the same evidence as before, a shell with no agent facet and no input from this view, and plain typing dismisses it and reaches the shell.

09

Resume, or launch

At rest the field reads Resume a session… and floats over ten chips, the last used first, each with its ⌥ key; the key launches that agent new, with the model and effort it was last launched with. Click the field or press ⌥↵ and typing searches the record: folder, agent, first prompt, branch and age, fuzzily, this folder's sessions first. Each result is two lines: the session's first prompt, then its model, branch and age. A two-way switch at the field's right, Here and All, keeps the results to this folder or opens them to every folder; Here is the default, and All puts this folder's sessions first and the rest under elsewhere. The highlighted one is a quiet fill; Enter picks it up in this pane through the provider's resume. The chips, the field and the list share the chip recipe from Part III: hairline rules, the ground behind them, a one-pixel lift.

Left: at rest, with Claude's chip under the pointer. Right: “fold” typed, the field and the results one card, the scope on All, so this folder's sessions come first and the rest follow under elsewhere; the highlighted one a quiet fill, and Enter would resume it here.

Decisions

decided One Half Light as shipped; the tightened five stay available as a named preset.

decided the legend is the pane's frame, and at rest its name keeps the pane colour.

decided the legend's form is the gauge edge, with the context meter in the bottom line.

decided stop, resume and fullscreen live in the stopwatch and the corner, outlined, as refined in §06 and specified in §07.

decided the active frame glows at the subtle level.

decided the empty pane is the prompt line with two jobs: the field resumes a past session, the chips launch a new agent.

deferred review, fork and lead. The composer bar is the likelier home; the launcher forecloses nothing.

decided a pane on another machine carries its OS mark before the name; absence means local.

for the build a query over the record for past sessions; effort names per provider on the launch descriptor; a per-agent memory of the last model and effort; the overlay's gate from the three pieces of evidence.

Locked 2026-09-04 and folded into the design system record as §07, beside the bar and the column. This page stays as the working record of how it was reached.
