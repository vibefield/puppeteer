Part I

The glyph bar

No title, no words. Counters as dots and numerals, views and tune as marks, the theme switch as it is. Every control on one recipe: 22px tall, 5px radius, 9px 700 numerals, 12px marks.

03

Marks and states

Four marks on a 24-unit grid, drawn monoline at 1.6 units, rendered at 12px in currentColor. Shown at three times size so the geometry can be judged. The tooltip is the skin's panel material at 8px.

Swarm: three bodies. List: three rules. Rain: three broken columns. Tune: two sliders. The counters keep the dot and the numeral, nothing else.

Rest · hover · on · focus, and the tooltip that hover raises after a beat. The on state is the hover pair held; focus is the skin's 1px outline.

Part II

The usage column

Each account is its mark inside its rings, one ring per usage window, with the tightest figure beneath. The tiles stack in one column down the stage's left edge, sorted by pressure, and scroll when there are more than fit. Hover or focus a tile and its popup opens to the right. Nine accounts below, one per ledger agent, in every state the code produces.

04

Today, at nine

For reference: the shipped component with nine entries, 243px tall. The letters are the fallback the app draws for six of the nine.

05

The tile

A 36px ring stack on a 46px tile. Rings sit in fixed slots from the mark outward, so the same slot means the same kind of window on every account: the short window innermost, the week next, any further limit outermost. A ring is the text colour; it turns orange at 80% or when the account is rate limited. Below: the tightest figure and its window name.

Left to right at three times size: Claude with three rings (5H · 7D · FABLE), Kimi with two, Antigravity rate limited, then the three states without data: sign in (dashed), not exposed (dotted), no limit data (faint).

06

The column at nine

Forty-six pixels wide, the stage's height less the slot's margins. In the column a tile is its rings and its figure; the window name and the reset move into the popup, so a tile is 44px tall and exactly four fit a 224px stage, with a count beneath them that says how many more; a wheel, a trackpad, or the arrow keys scroll it. The tightest account is always at the top.

Live: move over a tile, or tab to one. The Kimi popup is pinned open here so the record shows it. Rate limited and hot tiles read at the top; the three unmeasured states sit at the bottom of the scroll, where they cost nothing.

07

The popup

Opens to the right of its tile, top edges aligned, with a small connector pointing at the tile; it flips to the left only if the stage has no room. In the panel material with the inspector's blur. The account's mark and name, its state, then one row per window: the slot it occupies, a track, the figure, the reset. A footer carries the state detail: a retry countdown when rate limited, the fetch time otherwise.

At two times size: an available account, a rate-limited one, and the three unmeasured states, which say what to do instead of showing rows.

08

At fourteen

Every agent rosetta has a mark for: the nine in the ledger plus Cursor, Gemini CLI, Windsurf, Amp and Cline. The column's footprint does not change; the count at the bottom does.

Fourteen accounts, four in view, ten below the count. The optional rule still applies and is left for the build to decide: show only accounts with a live session by default, with an ALL toggle, and most sessions would fit without a scroll.

Part III

In the window

The column judged where it lives: a 640px-tall window at the default 35% stage, so the stage is 224px. The glyph bar above, three bodies and the runtime inspector on the stage, two terminal panes below. The bodies sit where the physics would leave them. The dashed outline is the column's footprint.

Nine accounts, four in view, the popup open to the right of the third. The whole top of the stage belongs to the swarm; the column takes a 46px strip of the left edge, a tenth of what the grid took.

46 × 207px of a 1100 × 224px stage. Hover any tile; the popup follows it to the right and never covers the column.

Decisions and what follows

for the build whether the column shows only accounts with a live session by default, and how the count chip behaves at the bottom of a scrolled list.

Then it lands in code: a vendored copy of the fourteen marks behind the existing icon component, the bar's one control recipe as a class in the skin, and the column as a new component in the chrome slot with its popup. The slot narrows to the column's width and takes the stage's height, so the swarm's obstacle shrinks with it. No terminal text is read anywhere; the readout keeps consuming the same usage batch it does today.
