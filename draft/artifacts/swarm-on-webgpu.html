Swarm on WebGPU

Godview · apps/godview · RFC 005 · 2026-09-06 · implemented, uncommitted

The swarm view now draws everything inside its stage on one WebGPU canvas: the field, every body, its light, its burn graph, its ring, its marks, and its type. The DOM keeps only what is not drawing. Physics stays in Matter.js; meaning stays in Core.

Every mark is an analytic signed-distance function of the same number, the distance to the rim, evaluated per pixel inside an instanced quad. A 34 px blur becomes one falloff; a ring, a dash, and a gauge become annulus and angle tests; the graph is a function of x read from a buffer. One render pass, five pipelines, one upload per frame, and the cost no longer grows with how many bodies are working or glowing.

Verified

  • Typecheck, 162 tests (33 new, pure: model, series, layout, colour, unread, parameters), esbuild bundle, format and package-layout gates all pass.
  • All five shaders compile clean on the Metal adapter of the pinned Electron (43, Chromium 150); WebGPU needs no flags on file://.
  • Live on an isolated profile, driven over the DevTools protocol with the repository's imposter running deep-research and permission scenarios: hollow, running with clock and comet, working with graph and activity line, blocked with light and kind line, the pane-link ring above the light, the unread dot, hold-to-create, drag, hover tooltip, both themes.
