The minimal pane, lit from inside: a flush line, a glow, the name.

From pass 1: the line now sits on the pane's edge with no gap, and the active pane glows from inside it. The brackets are gone from the name; nothing on the pane is bracketed now. The verbs, the model line and the context share are redrawn in two languages, one minimal and futuristic, one retro-future, and then assembled three ways. Read it in this order: the frame and its glow, then each part on its own, then the three compositions on the same three panes, on both grounds, with their states.
What changed
  1. The line is flush.1px in the pane colour at the pane's very edge, against the divider and the window. The badge grows out of it rather than hanging below it.
  2. The active pane glows from inside.A soft light in the pane colour bleeds inward from the line. Three shapes are offered; in one composition the glow also carries the context share.
  3. No brackets.The name badge drops the square brackets it had as drawn and is the name alone, with the marks before it. Model, effort, context and the verbs are set as a readout, a strip, a stamp or a meter.
  4. Two languages.Minimal-futuristic: hairlines, bare glyphs, a figure riding the tip of a meter. Retro-future: bare glyphs for the verbs, a grey bar, a stamped spec line, every corner rounded. Each composition commits to one.
  5. The terminal keeps its theme's ground.One Half Light in light, the Ghostty default in dark, as decided for §07. The window's glass no longer shows through the deck; the line, the badge and the keys sit on the terminal's own colour.
  6. Safe areas.The terminal's grid never enters the chrome's bands: 36px above, where the badge, the readout and the verbs share one centre line, and 32px below, where the figure, the stamp and the bar sit 11px up with the meter under them. What a TUI draws and what the frame draws never touch.
01

The frame: flush line, three glows

The same active pane three times, on both grounds. Halo lights every edge evenly, 34px deep. Lintel falls from the top edge only, as if the badge and the line above were the lamp. Corners blooms in the four corners and leaves the middle of the glass clear. All three appear with the line and go with it.

☼ Light: One Half Light, #fafafa.

◐ Dark: the Ghostty default, #282c34.

The parts

Each fact, redrawn without brackets

One corner of the active pane at 1.4×, with the pointer inside where the verbs are concerned. The crops follow the page theme; use the switch at the top to see them on the Ghostty default.

02

The verbs: stop, resume, fullscreen

Still pointer-only, still left of the badge, still growing out of the top edge. V1 keys are rounded like the console's badge, outlined in the pane colour with a 2px foot and the ground inside, hanging from the edge; the stop key carries the turn's time. V1b keycaps are the same keys set free, all four corners rounded, 4px below the edge. V2 the strip is one hairline bar whose cells are divided by hairlines, corners rounded to the badge's 5px. V3 ghost is the glyphs alone in the pane colour, with nothing drawn around them. After an exit the stop square becomes a green triangle and reads resume.

03

Model · effort

Lowercase, as the swarm sets it, in the terminal's family. M1 readout sits at the top-left in the ink at 50%, the pane's own status line. M2 stamp sits at the bottom-left, tracked wide at 9px, the way a model number is stamped on hardware. M3 with the name becomes a second cell in the badge, left of the brackets, so the corners stay empty. M4 under the name hangs beneath the badge in the pane colour.

04

Context share

Orange past 80%, always, so a hot pane reads before it is active. C1 the bar: a 64×4 grey bar with rounded ends, filled to the share in the ink, with the figure, bottom-right; it stays grey when hot and only gets heavier. C2 the edge: a 6px track along the bottom edge, the ink at 10%, fills to the share, so a low share still reads as a bar; the figure sits in the bottom-right corner above it. C3 the arc: a 14px ring beside the figure. C4 the glow: the inner glow's depth and strength are the share, the figure appears only under the pointer, and a hot pane glows orange; shown at 20, 62 and 88%.

☼ C4 on One Half Light: 20%, 62%, 88% active; the resting pane keeps the glow at 55%.

◐ C4 on the Ghostty default.

Variations

Rounded containers, a grey line, and the narrow window

Three variations on A and B, then both under a squeeze. The chrome is unchanged unless the section says otherwise.

08

A · Schematic, rounded

Only the selected pane is rounded. Its container takes an 8px radius, its line and glow follow it, and the pane's corner cuts the outer corner of its badge while the inner corner rounds to 5px to match. The resting panes stay rectangular, their badges square-cornered, with no margin and no divider between panes and the terminal's own background across the whole deck, so nothing shows through the corners. The 6px meter runs the full width of every pane, edge to edge; on the selected pane the corner's curve clips its ends.

11

The narrow window

A and B as single-pane windows at 330, 260, 200 and 150px, with the pointer inside so every verb is on screen, then at rest. The rules are container queries on the pane, not on the window. At every width the name takes at most half the pane before it yields to an ellipsis, and the readout or the stamp shrinks before the badge ever does. Under 300px the bar shortens to 40px and the readout hides while the pointer is inside so the verbs have its room. Under 220px the readout and the stamp hide, the stop verb keeps only its square, the bar shortens to 28px, and the terminal's side inset tightens to 14px while the safe areas above and below keep their height. The rounded variants follow the same rules.

Against the real TUIs

The chrome over what Codex and Claude Code actually draw, in the layout of the screenshot: Codex active on the left with the pointer inside, Claude Code at rest on the right, both just launched in rosetta. Codex writes a rounded box with the model and directory, a tips list, a full-width input band and a status line, and scrolls like a plain terminal, so its newest rows stay at the bottom when the pane is short. Claude Code writes its mascot, version, model and effort, the working directory, then a prompt box pinned to the bottom with the branch, the remote-control key and the mode under it. The terminal's grid is inset 36px above and 32px below, so the badge, the readout, the stamp and the bar sit in bands the TUI never draws into, and the pointer's verbs never cover a cell.

Two things the test shows. Both TUIs already print their model and effort, so the readout and the stamp repeat it, in a place that does not scroll away. And a fresh session sits under 10% of its context, so the meter is a short stub at the left while the figure stays in its corner.

Decision

Decided 2026-09-10: A · Schematic, with the selected pane rounded and the grey line. The frame keeps the reference's calm, every fact is exact, and the only box on the pane is the name's badge. Colour lives only in what says which pane is selected: the line, the glow, the badge and the verbs. The meter and its figure are always grey, heavier and bold past 80%, and the pane never turns orange. Only the selected pane rounds; the rest stay rectangular. The spec above is the pick; section 13 shows it as the overlay in VibeField.

Folded 2026-09-10: the composition now stands as §07 of the design-system record, replacing the card, legend, chips, corner and glow, with the safe areas, the narrow rules and the overlay sheet. In code the same day: Godview draws it (deck.css and the pane frame portalled into Ghosttea's panes); the record's §07 carries the build notes.
